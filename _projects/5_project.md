Today, we will start out by playing [Codenames](condenames.game). Why am I assigning this? The objective of the game is to categorize words. It is similar to the single player came connections that the NYT has every day. At the most basic level, neural networks are trying to classify an input X into an output Y. That's all they are.


In the second half, we are going to play the improv game "Word at a time story". Each person goes around adding one word to the story. This is what a decoder only language model is doing. This is GPT.
